Mount Everest in the Himalayas is the highest mountain above sea level at about 8,849 metres. It lies on the border between Nepal and Tibet in China. The Himalayas formed as the Indian plate collided with the Eurasian plate, and they are still rising slowly.

The Amazon rainforest in South America is the largest tropical rainforest on Earth, mostly in Brazil. The Amazon River running through it is about 6,400 kilometres long and carries more water than any other river.

The Sahara in North Africa is the largest hot desert in the world, covering about 9 million square kilometres, roughly the size of the United States. Much of it is rocky plateau and gravel plain, and only a part is sand dunes.

Hyderabad is the capital of the Indian state of Telangana. It was founded in 1591 by Muhammad Quli Qutb Shah, who also built the Charminar. The city is known for Hussain Sagar lake, Hyderabadi biryani and a large IT industry centred around HITEC City.

The Pacific Ocean is the largest and deepest ocean, covering about a third of the Earth's surface. Its Mariana Trench holds the deepest known point, Challenger Deep, at nearly 11,000 metres below sea level.

The Nile in north-east Africa is about 6,650 kilometres long and is often named the longest river in the world, although some measurements give that title to the Amazon. It flows north through Egypt into the Mediterranean Sea and supported ancient Egyptian civilization.

There are seven continents: Asia, Africa, North America, South America, Antarctica, Europe and Australia, also called Oceania. Asia is the largest by area and population, and Antarctica is the coldest and driest.

Japan is an island country in East Asia made up of four main islands, Honshu, Hokkaido, Kyushu and Shikoku. Its capital is Tokyo, and its highest mountain is Mount Fuji at 3,776 metres.
