To make masala chai, boil water with crushed ginger and cardamom, add loose tea leaves and simmer for about two minutes. Pour in milk and sugar, let it come to a boil and rise once or twice, then strain it into cups.

To cook fluffy basmati rice, rinse the rice until the water runs mostly clear and soak it for 20 minutes. Use about one cup of rice to one and a half cups of water, bring to a boil, cover and cook on low heat for 12 to 15 minutes, then rest it with the lid on for five minutes.

Hyderabadi biryani uses the dum method. Marinated meat is layered with partly cooked basmati rice, fried onions, herbs and saffron milk in a heavy pot, which is sealed with dough or foil and cooked slowly so the steam blends all the flavours.

Most adults need seven to nine hours of sleep a night. Sleep helps the brain store memories and the body repair itself. A regular bedtime, a dark cool room and less screen time before bed all make sleep better.

Staying hydrated is important for energy and concentration. Needs vary with weather, activity and body size, but many adults take roughly two to three litres of fluids a day from drinks and food. Pale yellow urine is a simple sign of good hydration.

Protein is made of amino acids and helps build and repair muscles and other tissues. Good sources include dal, beans, eggs, paneer, curd, fish, chicken and tofu. This is general information and not medical advice.

Washing hands with soap for at least 20 seconds removes germs and helps prevent illness. Wet your hands, lather the fronts, backs, between the fingers and under the nails, then rinse and dry them.

Vitamin D helps the body absorb calcium for strong bones. The skin makes it when exposed to sunlight, and small amounts come from foods such as eggs and fortified milk. Low vitamin D is common, so a doctor can test it if you are worried.
